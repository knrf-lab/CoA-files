How to use this binder

  1. Learn in order, 1 → 8. Each tab starts from zero and builds up. Read the theory, then work every Example box with a pencil before reading its solution.
  2. Close each tab with its "Check yourself" box. If you can answer those without looking, you have the tab.
  3. Connect to the cheat sheet. Every tab header lists its cheat-sheet boxes (T4) and the quiz/practice cards that use it (Q4, P4). After a tab, redo those cards on the cheat sheet. The words on the back of the cheat sheet are summaries of the pages here.
  4. Test yourself with Tab Q, then mark it with Tab K. Every K answer links back to the page with the method.
  5. In the exam: classify the question with Tab I, then use the cheat sheet. Show the formula and the substitution, the way the prof's quiz solutions do.
✔ Every number in the examples and the answer key was recomputed by computer. The MARIE programs were run in a small MARIE simulator and every cache trace was replayed. Where a slide has a mistake, the corrected fact is used and the slip is listed in Tab X.

Sources: the lecture decks S1 Introduction, S2–S3 MARIE, S4–S5 Instruction Set Architectures, S6–S7 Memory (Dr. Endra, based on Null 2023), the prof's UTS Study Guide, the quiz and the prof's quiz solutions.
